Frontend engineer at [AhnLab](https://www.ahnlab.com), a security company in Seoul.

I work mostly in Vue/Nuxt and TypeScript. What I write about tends to be whatever
turned out to be harder than the documentation implied — micro frontends over
Module Federation, SSR hydration, and the parts of frontend CI/CD that are not
really about CI/CD.

Most posts here are written in Korean first. The English side is a rewrite rather
than a translation, so it is shorter and it lags behind. If a post shows **EN** in
grey at the top, the English version does not exist yet.
